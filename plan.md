# Urban Pulse Hotel Website - Development Plan

## Project Overview
**Template Name:** Urban Pulse  
**Target:** City Center Business Hotels, Design Hotels, Trendy Boutique Hotels  
**Location:** India (Mumbai/Delhi city center)  
**Price Range:** Mid-range (₹4,500 - ₹12,000 per night)  

## Design Concept
- **Color Scheme:** Deep navy (#1a1a2e) with electric blue (#16a8ff) and neon yellow (#ffd700) accents
- **Style:** Bold, dynamic, energetic with asymmetric layouts
- **Typography:** Bold, modern fonts
- **Elements:** Geometric shapes, video backgrounds, interactive elements
